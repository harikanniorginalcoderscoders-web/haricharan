ABOUT SMARTHOMZ

A more thoughtful way to discover smart living.

Technology can be exciting, but choosing the right technology starts with understanding what it does and how it fits into everyday life.

OUR PURPOSE

Make discovery simpler.

SMARTHOMZ is a place to explore smart-home devices, learn what they do, and compare their useful features and limitations before deciding what belongs in your home.

OUR APPROACH

Clarity over complexity.

We focus on product information, practical descriptions and straightforward exploration. The goal is to help visitors understand technology, not pressure them into a purchase.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND

Connected home ideas.

Explore lighting, home security, automation, entertainment, sensors and other categories. Product listings come from the SMARTHOMZ database.

OUR PRINCIPLE

Your home, your choices.

Learn about a product's capabilities, features, advantages and trade-offs so you can make informed decisions for your needs.
